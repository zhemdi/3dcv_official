R, t map left-camera coordinates to right-camera coordinates: Xr = R Xl + t, in the OpenCV camera frame (x right, y down, z along the optical axis). Pixels: u right, v down, origin top-left, 640×480 image, K = [[f,0,cx],[0,f,cy],[0,0,1]]. E = [t]×R, F = K−TE K−1, and urTF ul = 0.
